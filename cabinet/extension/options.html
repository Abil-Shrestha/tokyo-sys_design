Save to Cabinet

Connect this extension to the Cabinet app on your computer. In Cabinet, open Settings → Browser extension and copy the address and key.

  • Click the toolbar button to save the page you're on (with a snapshot).
  • Right-click a page, link, image, video or selected text → Save … to Cabinet.
  • Hold ⌥ Option / Alt and click any image.
  • Press ⌥⇧S to save the current page.
